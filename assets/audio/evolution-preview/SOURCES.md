Evolution preview sound sources

The standard evolution cue set uses four CC0 assets. They are bundled locally so the preview does not depend on third-party hosting at runtime.

- charge.ogg — power_up_sound_v1.ogg by Julie Damsgaard / Spring Spring / Spring Enterprises. CC0. Played at 2.15x to fit the preview's short auto-evolution cycle. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/power-up-sound-effects
- flip-whoosh.mp3 — MP3-encoded copy of swish-7.wav from Swishes Sound Pack by artisticdude. The original WAV is retained as flip-whoosh.wav. CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
- evolution-flash.ogg — magical_6.ogg from Magic Spell SFX by JaggedStone. CC0; no attribution required. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/magic-spell-sfx
- landing.mp3 — jumpland44100.mp3 by MentalSanityOff (submitted by qubodup). CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/jump-landing-sound

MEGA evolution has a separate three-cue palette and does not reuse the standard evolution flip, flash, or landing sounds:

- mega-charge-whoosh.mp3 — “Magic Whoosh” by DustyWind. CC0. Bundled from the high-quality MP3 preview. Source: https://freesound.org/people/DustyWind/sounds/715784/
- mega-orb-shatter.mp3 — “glass-explosion_01.ogg” by C_Rogers. CC0. Bundled from the high-quality MP3 preview. Source: https://freesound.org/people/C_Rogers/sounds/203371/
- mega-reveal.ogg — magical_4.ogg by JaggedStone. CC0; no attribution required. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/magic-spell-sfx
