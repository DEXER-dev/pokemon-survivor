# Lugia boss attack sounds

Original procedural synthesis created for this project, licensed under the project's 0BSD license.
No third-party recordings or Pokémon cries are sampled.

Rebuild with `python tools/generate-lugia-sfx.py` (requires FFmpeg).
Seven mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis clips: soft charge, pressure blast, short wing gust,
rolling tidal wave, fast sweep and resonant cross-current with a brighter second volley. Peaks are normalized
to 0.70 before encoding; the combat SFX bus controls playback gain.
