# Lugia opening audio sources

Both short effects used by the Whirl Islands intro are CC0 recordings downloaded from Freesound. They are loaded locally by `CombatSfx` and played as a two-cue sequence: a water-rise whoosh at the start, then a splash on Lugia's reveal. `?mute=1` disables loading and playback for silent testing.

- `water-emerge-whoosh.mp3` — Morgan V., [Freesound 389987](https://freesound.org/people/morganveilleux/sounds/389987/), CC0 1.0.
- `water-splash.mp3` — AardsReal, [Freesound 842165](https://freesound.org/people/AardsReal/sounds/842165/), CC0 1.0.
